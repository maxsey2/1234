Welcome to Countdown to Christmas

Countdown to Christmas is your festive destination to celebrate the magic of the holiday season. Our mission is to bring joy and excitement as we count down the days together. Check out the live countdown and enjoy the festive spirit with us!

Our story

Countdown to Christmas was created to enhance the spirit of the holidays with a dynamic countdown timer, festive music, and beautiful snowfall effects.

What we offer

A live countdown timer updated every second, cheerful music to brighten your day, and interactive snowfall that follows your cursor.
